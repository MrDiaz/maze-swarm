Maze Swarm combat sounds: sources and licenses
================================================

Clips in Assets/Maze/Resources/Audio are cut by Tools/prep_sfx.py (trimmed, mono 44.1 kHz,
peak-normalized to -1 dBFS, faded). The source files on the T7 drive are not modified.

Gunshots, pump and reload handling: "The Free Firearm Sound Library"
  (opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library), by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson,
  Kevin Heras and Matthew Nanney. License: CC0 (public domain). Downloaded and renamed by Manuel;
  on the T7 drive at /Volumes/T7 Shield/Assets/Sounds/Realistic Weapons.
  rifle_1, rifle_2         AK-47/C_31P.wav (AK-47 7.62x39, mid distance), shots at 0.33 s and 4.40 s
  rifle_3                  AK-47/C_28P.wav (AK-47, near), shot at 0.61 s
  smg_1..3                 Carl Gustav M45/G_31P.wav (Swedish K 9 mm SMG, near), shots at 0.31/3.50/6.72 s
  shotgun_1, shotgun_2     Nova/O_17P.wav (Benelli Nova 12 ga pump, mid distance), shots at 0.68/3.70 s
  shotgun_3                Model 12/K_17P.wav (Winchester Model 12 12 ga pump, mid distance), shot at 0.91 s
  shotgun_pump_1           Model 12/K_22P.wav, rack clicks at 4.19-4.35 s and 4.69-4.88 s, spliced closer
  shotgun_pump_2           Model 12/K_17P.wav, rack clicks at 3.07-3.21 s and 3.89-4.05 s, spliced closer
  dry_click                PPSh/P_18P.wav 29.71-29.79 s (handling click between bursts)
  mag_out                  PPSh/P_18P.wav 27.62-27.80 s
  mag_in                   PPSh/P_18P.wav 9.41-9.50 s
  shell_in                 Model 12/K_22P.wav 3.27-3.40 s
  (The library has no dedicated reload takes; these are mechanical clicks recorded between shots.)

Wet sounds: "Forge & Fantasy - Weapon SFX Pack" by Case Portman Audio (IMPACTS/Juicy)
  squish_1..4              Impact_Juicy_05, 07, 09, 10
  hit_1..3                 Arrow_Impact_Flesh_02, 05, 06
  drip_1..3                Arrow_Impact_Flesh_07, 08, 09 (pitched up 30%, 140 ms)
  License: royalty-free, personal and commercial use, editing allowed, no attribution required;
  the sounds may not be resold or redistributed as standalone audio or as a sound pack
  (see license.txt in the pack).

Goo layer of the Drone death: "Squish Sounds Effects" by EZduzziteh
  https://opengameart.org/content/squish-sounds-effects  (downloaded Oct 10, 2026 to Sounds/squish)
  goo_1..6                 squish_01_0, squish_02 .. squish_06 (MP3, trimmed to 320 ms)
  goo_7                    squishpop (250 ms)
  squishsplat_impact.mp3 was downloaded but is not used (the arrow-flesh hits read better).
  License: CC0 (public domain), no attribution required; credited here anyway.

Each Drone death plays one squish_* (juicy impact) plus one goo_* about 3 dB quieter.

Bloater (Forge & Fantasy and the squish pack, pitched down):
  bloater_burst            Impact_Juicy_05 at 0.6x pitch (death burst)
  bloater_spawn            squish_06 (EZduzziteh, CC0) at 0.72x pitch (a sac bursting with minis)
  bloater_step             IMPACTS/Body/Impact_Body_07 at 0.62x pitch (footfall thud)

  License: ElevenLabs output, commercial use allowed on a paid plan (check the plan the clips were made on).
