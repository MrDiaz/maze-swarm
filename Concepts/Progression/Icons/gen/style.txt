STYLE (one consistent icon family): game UI icons for a mobile sci-fi shooter whose menus are blue holographic panels on a dropship bridge (see the attached menu screenshot for mood and palette). Bold, simple, chunky, centered symbols that read instantly at 64 px on a phone. Clean semi-flat painted rendering with a light bevel and soft inner shading, crisp silhouettes, slight cyan/blue glow. No text, no letters, no numbers, no frames, no card borders, no backgrounds behind the symbols, no drop-shadow on the floor.
LAYOUT: a single square 1024x1024 image arranged as a 2x2 grid of four separate icons. Each icon is centered in its own quadrant (512x512) and takes up about 60-65% of the quadrant, with generous empty padding; nothing touches or crosses the quadrant lines or the image edges. Do NOT draw grid lines or dividers. Background everywhere is plain flat pure white (#FFFFFF), no gradient, no vignette, no texture. All four icons share the same rendering style, line weight, lighting from the top-left and the same scale.
